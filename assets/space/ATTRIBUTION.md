# Space texture credits

Planet, Moon, Sun, cloud, and Saturn ring textures are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) and are used
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The maps are based on and color tuned from spacecraft imagery and planetary
data, including NASA imagery. Files are stored locally so the Space Explorer
does not make third party requests while a child is using it.

The interior layer textures are original procedural artwork made for this
website. They are educational visualizations rather than measured subsurface
photographs.
